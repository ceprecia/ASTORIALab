# Illustrative advisor agenda

A proposed **20-minute** discussion for the fictional first-year Materials Science student at Term 1 Week 7. No meeting date is supplied or booked. This is preparation, not minutes or an agreement by the advisor.

**Desired outcome:** Agree on useful next work within four hours per week while clarifying the evidence and approvals still needed.

| Minutes | Topic and sources | Natural question | Desired result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 | Rotation records and training, H2–H3; E1–E2 | “What is still needed for the second rotation record and practical clearance?” | A clear account of the missing evidence and who can establish it. |
| 5–10 | Portfolio, H4; E4 | “What should I finish first to make this portfolio sufficient by Week 10?” | A realistic draft scope and a plan for obtaining the office receipt after submission. |
| 10–15 | Project brief and independent work, H2, H6; E1, E3, E5 | “What is still needed before I can start independent sample preparation in Week 8?” | Useful desk-based work, a clear approval and clearance path, and an explicit distinction between the Week 8 preference and permission. |
| 15–20 | Review and home-lab choice, H5; E2, E4–E5 | “What must be recorded before the Week 12 review and a later home-lab decision?” | A shared understanding of rotation records, portfolio receipt, review outcome and capacity confirmation. |

Bring the [working-document and evidence descriptions](../inputs/completion_evidence.md) and the [illustrative reconciliation](illustrative_milestone_plan.md). The requested decisions remain open until the relevant people respond. The agenda does not establish completion, sign-off, an approved start or a laboratory placement.

## Continue the example

Next, add a fictional second rotation sign-off or portfolio receipt and reassess the review prerequisites. Keep each new record distinct from the interpretation it supports.
