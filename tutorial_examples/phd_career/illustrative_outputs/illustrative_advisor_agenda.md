# Illustrative advisor agenda

Fictional meeting preparation for 2028-01-24, 25 minutes. This is an agenda example, not meeting minutes, an invitation or an agreement by the advisor.

**Desired outcome:** A bounded memo plan, a committee nomination path and a realistic decision about the proposed April review.

| Minutes | Topic and source | Concrete question | Useful result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 | Memo outline, E2–E3; H2 | “Which parts of this outline are essential for the progress memo, and what would make the draft sufficient?” | Agreed scope and feedback expectations. |
| 5–13 | Committee requirements, H3; E4 | “Who could fill the remaining seat, which members count as core faculty, and how early should I send the complete approval request?” | Candidate names to approach and the correct approval path, not assumed approval. |
| 13–21 | Proposal sequence, H4; E2 | “Given the undrafted proposal and five-hour weekly budget, is April 12 a feasible target? What should be ready first?” | Retain or revise the target, with work dependencies. |
| 21–25 | Evidence and follow-up, H2–H6 | “What submission and outcome acknowledgments should I retain, and what next check-in would be useful?” | Proposed next actions and missing evidence to confirm. |

Bring the [memo outline and advisor note](../inputs/completion_evidence.md) and the [requirement reconciliation](illustrative_milestone_plan.md). If a question cannot be answered, record it as unresolved. Do not turn a suggested candidate, a tentative date or this illustrative agenda into a commitment.

## Continue the example
