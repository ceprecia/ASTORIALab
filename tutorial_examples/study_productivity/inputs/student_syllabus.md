## What the course teaches

By the end, a learner should be able to locate a place on a grid, distinguish overall direction from a walkable route, calculate route distance, apply a closure rule, and explain which conclusions the supplied map supports.

## Four-week outline

| Week | Dates | Topic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | February 3 to 7 | Grid coordinates, directions, distance and closed edges |
| 2 | February 10 to 14 | Comparing routes under different map rules |
| 3 | February 17 to 21 | Checking another person's route explanation |
| 4 | February 24 to 28 | Writing and defending a route commentary |

Only Week 1 lesson content is supplied in this tutorial. Later topics establish the fictional course schedule.

## Working policies

- Use the supplied map rules when answering an assessment. State any extra assumption explicitly.
- Show the route as a sequence of cells and give distance units.
- For this fictional course, an assistant may explain the lesson, ask practice questions and critique a draft. The learner checks the reasoning and writes the submitted explanation.
- Extension decisions belong to the fictional instructor. This tutorial contains no individual exceptions.
- Supplied lesson content controls the Week 1 answer key. Missing speed or delay information remains missing.
