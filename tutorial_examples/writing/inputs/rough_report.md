# Rough seedling report

This is a deliberately flawed draft for an original fictional exercise. Its unsupported statements are errors to find, not source facts. All data are synthetic.

## R1: Question

We tested whether more water makes all plants healthier and more productive.

## R2: Methods

We randomly assigned eight seedlings to receive either 20 or 40 mL of water daily for 14 days. Investigators were blinded and all environmental conditions were held constant. We measured growth at the start and end of the experiment.

## R3: Results

The 20 mL group increased in height by a mean of 3.3 cm, whereas the 40 mL group increased by 4.1 cm. Doubling the water doubled growth. The difference was statistically significant and demonstrated that water caused healthier plants.

## R4: Conclusion

This experiment proves that 40 mL per day is the optimal watering schedule for seedlings. Gardeners should adopt this schedule to increase crop yield. A larger experiment would confirm the same result.
