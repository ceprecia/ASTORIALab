# Illustrative research plan

This is one possible output, supplied for comparison. It is not the result of
running an assistant or a human experiment.

## Question and prediction

The exercise asks whether a quiet break changes proofreading detections in
comparison with continuous reading. A positive break-minus-continuous difference
is the provisional prediction, not an established effect.

## Proposed comparison

Each fictional participant contributes a detection count in both conditions.
Calculate the paired difference and report the condition means and the number
of planted errors available. Describe false flags separately as counts.

## What needs a design decision

Passage difficulty, passage-to-condition assignment and possible carryover are
not resolved. Balanced block order alone does not resolve those issues. The
sample of eight invented records does not justify an actual study's sample size.

A useful next step is to specify passage assignment and a procedure that limits
carryover before making an actual recruitment or analysis plan.
