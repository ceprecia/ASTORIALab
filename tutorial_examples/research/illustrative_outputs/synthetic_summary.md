# Illustrative descriptive summary

These are arithmetic summaries of invented records, not empirical findings.

| Quantity | Continuous reading | Quiet break |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Paired records | 8 | 8 |
| Correct detections, total | 60 of 96 planted errors | 67 of 96 planted errors |
| Correct detections, mean per passage | 7.500 of 12 | 8.375 of 12 |
| Detected fraction | 62.50% | 69.79% |
| False flags, total | 6 | 6 |

The paired detection differences are `2, 1, 1, 1, 1, 0, 2, -1`.
Their mean is `7 / 8 = 0.875` additional detections per 12-error passage,
equivalent to 7.29 percentage points of the planted errors.

Six records have a positive difference, one is tied and one is negative.
Equal false-flag totals do not establish equal false-positive rates, because
the relevant error-free opportunity counts are unknown.

The invented break counts are higher descriptively. They cannot establish that
breaks improve performance in a population. Passage differences, assignment
and carryover remain design concerns. No significance test was performed.
