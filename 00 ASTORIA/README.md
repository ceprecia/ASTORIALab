# ASTORIA starter

Open the extracted starter folder in your assistant and grant access to its files. If you want to add ASTORIA to an existing Obsidian vault, show the assistant that vault too. Say:

> Set up my vault

For first-time setup, choose **Start with the basics** for Research Assistant, Janitor and ASTORIA System Builder, or **Help me choose** to select additional systems and skills. Review the proposed plan, then open your working vault in Obsidian.

For updates, show the assistant your working ASTORIA vault and the new starter folder. Use the same command. The assistant applies the update while preserving your choices and custom work, then audits for needed repairs. Review proposed repairs before they are applied.

The complete [manual setup guide](skills/setup-my-vault/references/manual_setup.md) is included for offline use. The online [tutorial](https://github.com/ceprecia/ASTORIALab/blob/main/tutorial.md) introduces ASTORIA and links to optional worked examples. Keep the complete [LICENSE](LICENSE) with the starter and any files you reuse.
