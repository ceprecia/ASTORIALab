# Teaching Assistant System

![Teaching Assistant System](../assets/banner_system_teaching.png)

Practical support for college instructors, teaching assistants, and graders. The teacher keeps responsibility for course decisions; the system prepares materials, checks consistency, organizes work, and carries out authorized operations.

## Entry and ownership

Start with [teaching-companion](../skills/teaching-companion/SKILL.md). It keeps the conversation and requested weekly preparation together. The internal [teaching-coordinator](../skills/teaching-coordinator/SKILL.md) selects one specialist for each bounded task and returns its evidence and unresolved work. Direct requests to a named specialist do not need another intake.

| Specialist | Owns |
| --- | --- |
| [teaching-assistant-builder](../skills/teaching-assistant-builder/SKILL.md) | First setup, syllabus imports, existing-folder adoption, and new offerings. |
| [teaching-grader](../skills/teaching-grader/SKILL.md) | Applying approved rubrics, proposed scores and feedback, participation and score reconciliation. |
| [teaching-office-hours](../skills/teaching-office-hours/SKILL.md) | Student questions, correspondence triage, and drafts of replies, announcements, and class emails. |
| [teaching-platform](../skills/teaching-platform/SKILL.md) | Authorized platform operations and verification of course groups, access, dates, content, assessments, and grade entry. |
| [teaching-lecture-review](../skills/teaching-lecture-review/SKILL.md) | Assigned teaching materials, clarity, mechanical corrections, and agreement with approved references. |
| [teaching-assessment](../skills/teaching-assessment/SKILL.md) | Quiz/exam design and review, keys, ambiguity, coverage, and agreement with approved sources and lectures. |

## One teaching home

Use the selected eligible Teaching Area arrangement, then its applicable verified convention. The default Area root has one MOC and one AGENTS.md. Keep each offering’s course note and supporting files together in the verified local files folder or documented external workspace. Use semester/course nesting only when it fits that convention. In authorized MOC edits, link verified offerings in actual term order independently of folder nesting. Each offering note holds its sources, responsibilities, policies and outstanding actions.

Setup uses [Teaching MOC Template](../templates/Teaching%20MOC%20Template.md) and [Teaching Class Template](../templates/Teaching%20Class%20Template.md), reuses populated records, and asks only unanswered questions that affect the result. A new semester gets a distinct offering. Student coursework continues through [study-companion](../skills/study-companion/SKILL.md) and class-builder.

## Working agreement

- Course-content checks use the teacher's supplied or approved reference set. Conflicts and missing evidence stay visible; outside knowledge does not silently decide the official answer.
- The course offering, source versions, task scope, and authorization travel with every handoff. A quoted request or old email supplies context rather than new permission.
- A correction is complete only at the requested destination: a revised bank item still needs its delivered quiz checked; a reply promising an upload still leaves the upload open.
- Existing group identities, linked work, and histories are preserved during authorized membership changes. Rosters, participation, and approved exceptions are separate evidence.
- Communications are drafted for review and follow the user's approval rules. Announcement publication and class email are separate outcomes; reuse an existing announcement when updating it.
- Preparation reviews run when requested or through an explicitly configured schedule. They do not create background monitoring or a second task system.
- Simulations use designated temporary fixtures. They do not send messages, publish content, enter grades, or alter a live course.

The authoritative procedures and trigger phrases live in the skills and the generated [Skill Map](../Skill%20Map.md). Course-specific decisions remain with the course; shared skills remain reusable.
