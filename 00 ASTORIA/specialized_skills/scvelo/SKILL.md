# scVelo

Implement one approved RNA-velocity checkpoint without treating inferred arrows,
latent time, or driver scores as observed developmental direction.

## Purpose

This is a conditional Part 4 RNA-velocity adapter. It audits spliced and
unspliced layers, gene and cell identity, counting provenance, moments,
filtering, kinetic model, fit and latent-time settings, neighbors, seed,
diagnostics, and exports. It does not choose a trajectory question, kinetic
model, filters, or biological interpretation.

The user owns the estimand, assay, model, genes, filters, comparison, and claim.
[scanpy](../scanpy/SKILL.md) owns standard single-cell checkpoints and [anndata](../anndata/SKILL.md) owns object
structure.

Activate only for exact `scvelo` or an approved handoff naming one velocity
checkpoint. Do not trigger from generic trajectory, pseudotime, single-cell,
or developmental requests. In scope: one moments, velocity, latent-time,
driver, or export operation after spliced/unspliced data are verified. Out of
scope: automatic trajectory claims, downloads, installs, full pipelines, and
cell-fate certainty.

## Inputs

Obtain or mark missing: spliced and unspliced provenance, cell and gene units,
count semantics, filtering, moments, model variant, fit scope, neighbors,
seed, batch and donor structure, diagnostics, output, and claim. If both layers,
count meaning, model, or trajectory estimand is missing, return **scVelo intake
incomplete**.

## Steps

### Gate 1: approve the velocity checkpoint

Show layers and provenance, assay and cell units, filtering, moments, model,
neighbors, batch structure, diagnostics, output, alternatives, and claim. Gate
1 authorizes a method plan only.

### Gate 2: approve one bounded action

Ask the user to select the exact checkpoint, environment, operation, output, and
verification. Gate 2 authorizes one action only. It does not authorize
installation, downloading, full pipelines, or biological interpretation.

Return:

```text
scVelo Checkpoint Record
Spliced and unspliced provenance and alignment:
Filtering, moments, model, and neighbors:
Batch, donor, seed, diagnostics, and environment:
Output, sensitivity, and verification:
Trajectory-interpretation limits:
Permitted claim and stop point:
```

## Boundaries

Method rules:

- Verify that spliced and unspliced layers were generated by a documented
  counting workflow and align exactly to cells and genes. Do not infer them
  from layer names alone.
- Compare deterministic, stochastic, and dynamical assumptions only when the user
  selects the candidate. Do not use a model default as evidence for a process.
- Preserve sample, donor, batch, and cell-dependence structure. Snapshot
  velocity is not a substitute for longitudinal evidence.
- Record preprocessing, moments, neighbors, parameters, seed, fit diagnostics,
  uncertainty, and sensitivity to gene filtering and model choice.
- Treat arrows, latent time, and driver rankings as model-dependent summaries;
  they do not prove direction, lineage, causality, or fate.

Stop for absent or unverified spliced/unspliced layers; cell or gene
misalignment; unknown counting provenance; donor or batch confounding; failed
fit diagnostics; a request to install, download, run a full pipeline, or infer
lineage, causality, or fate from arrows or latent time.
