# PPMI facts that control the review (ppmi-skill)

Read this reference when the skill or the user asks for it.

## PPMI facts that control the review

Use these as dated orientation and recheck them at invocation:

- PPMI began recruitment in 2010 as the Parkinson's Progression Markers
  Initiative. The current program and agreement use Parkinson's Precision
  Medicine Initiative. Naming does not make old and current protocols
  interchangeable.
- The current study includes primary enrollment cohorts for Parkinson disease,
  prodromal Parkinson disease, and healthy controls. Eligibility criteria,
  visit schedules, protocols, and assessments have changed over time.
- Participants are also assigned biomarker-informed cohorts and subgroups
  after enrollment. Enrollment cohort, current clinical status, and analytic
  subgroup are separate variables. The Curated Data Cut and Groups and
  Subgroups Guidance Document define recommended current analytic groupings.
- Some people now in the Parkinson disease cohort were recruited through a
  legacy SWEDD cohort and later developed Parkinson disease. Some prodromal
  enrollees already had a Parkinson diagnosis at the baseline visit. Do not
  infer one immutable group label.
- The public dashboard accessed 2026-07-23 displayed 7,755 enrolled
  participants: 2,864 in the Parkinson disease cohort, 4,317 prodromal, and
  574 healthy controls. These are dated aggregate enrollment counts and may
  change.
- Qualified researchers can apply for individual-level clinical, imaging,
  omics, genetic, sensor, and biomarker data. Exact product availability is
  participant-, visit-, protocol-, and request-specific.
- Current participant guidance describes motor, cognitive, neuropsychiatric,
  olfactory, DaTscan, MRI, fluid, DNA, and other assessments. Diffusion MRI
  and other modalities are available only for subsets. Do not assume every
  participant has every modality or visit.
- The standard database is updated daily, made available as collected, and
  described as not curated. Existing elements may change with correction,
  disease progression, and new analysis methods. A reproducible design must
  pin the extraction date, exact files or tables, query, and data cut.
- Standard access requires a signed Data Use Agreement, online application,
  and compliance with the Publication Policy. The Data and Publications
  Committee reviews applications.
- Raw BAM or FASTQ products require an active account and separate Genetic
  Data Request. Some alpha-synuclein seeding-amplification results for
  prodromal and healthy-control participants are sequestered and require study
  leadership approval.
- Biospecimens, data, genetic products, sequestered products, and cell lines
  have different request, review, agreement, material-transfer, return-of-
  results, and unblinding routes. This skill handles only public planning.
- DUA 5.0 prohibits reidentification or contact, disclosure beyond the
  approved application, and use of noncontained AI tools. Team members who
  use or receive data must register and agree to the terms.
- The DUA requires annual updates, institutional safeguards, current
  publication procedures, acknowledgement, administrative manuscript review,
  and compliance with PPMI intellectual-property and return-of-generated-data
  requirements.
- Models trained on participant-level PPMI data may be shared only through
  MJFF-approved methods under DUA 5.0. Removing subject codes or altering data
  does not create ordinary redistribution permission.
