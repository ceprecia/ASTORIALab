# ADHD-200 facts that control the review (adhd200-skill)

Read this reference when the skill or the user asks for it.

## ADHD-200 facts that control the review

Use these as dated orientation and recheck them at invocation:

- The original ADHD-200 release reports 776 anatomical and resting-state fMRI
  datasets from eight independent imaging sites: 491 typically developing
  participants and 285 participants diagnosed with ADHD, ages 7 to 21.
- The competition later added previously unlabeled test records. The official
  ADHD-200 Preprocessed landing page describes 947 labeled datasets: 585
  typically developing participants and 362 participants with ADHD. Bellec
  et al. describe 973 individuals when 26 records with unknown diagnostic
  status are included. These are different diagnostic and product grains and
  must not be substituted for the original 776-person release.
- The source is multisite retrospective aggregation, not one harmonized
  prospective protocol. Sites differ in scanner, acquisition, rest
  instructions, diagnostic instruments, recruitment, available phenotypes,
  class composition, and missingness.
- The imaging modalities are T1-weighted structural MRI and resting-state
  fMRI. ADHD-200 is not a task-fMRI dataset.
- Phenotype fields and diagnostic codes vary by site. Diagnoses and subtype
  labels reflect DSM-IV or DSM-IV-TR-era instruments and must not be silently
  relabeled under current criteria.
- Some sites contributed only controls to the original training release.
  Training-versus-test and site-versus-diagnosis structure can be confounded.
- Official correction history includes a 2011 structural voxel-dimension fix
  affecting 73 participants. The exact source package and correction state
  must be verified.
- Raw releases and PCP derivatives are distinct. PCP pipelines, atlases,
  nuisance regressors, filters, scrubbing states, and output availability vary
  and are not interchangeable.
- Official FCP/INDI materials describe NITRC registration and unrestricted
  noncommercial research use. Current terms and download route must be
  verified before any access action.
- The competition itself cautioned that imaging classification was not ready
  for clinical use.

Do not treat diagnosis labels as clinical ground truth, a derivative manifest
as a complete raw cohort, or one site's acquisition and phenotype schema as
universal.
