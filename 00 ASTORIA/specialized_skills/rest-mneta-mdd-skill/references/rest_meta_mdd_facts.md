## REST-meta-MDD facts that control the review

Use these as dated orientation and recheck them at invocation:

- DIRECT began in 2017. REST-meta-MDD Phase I pooled 25 previously collected
  study cohorts from 17 participating hospitals in China.
- The source describes 2,428 contributed datasets from 1,300 people with MDD
  and 1,128 normal controls. It calls each contributed cohort a `site` for
  convenience. Cohort count, hospital count, and scanner site must not be
  treated as one grain.
- The consortium shared final resting-state fMRI indices produced locally,
  not a centralized raw rs-fMRI archive. A standardized DPARSF-based protocol
  reduced processing variability, but local acquisition and implementation
  differences remain.
- The shared resource is not source BIDS and must not be represented as raw
  NIfTI ready for a new preprocessing pipeline. Exact deposited derivative
  files and phenotypes must be verified in a DUA-compliant contained
  environment after separate authorization.
- The public cohort table documents heterogeneous scanners, coils, TR, TE,
  flip angles, slice geometry, time points, voxel sizes, and sample sizes.
  One generic 3 T or five-to-ten-minute acquisition description is false.
- Among 1,300 MDD cases, 562 were described as first episode, 282 as
  recurrent, and episode status was unavailable for 456.
- Among the 562 first-episode cases, 318 were first-episode drug-naive, 160
  were scanned while receiving antidepressants, and medication status was
  unavailable for 84.
- Among the 282 recurrent cases, 121 were scanned while receiving
  antidepressants, 76 were not receiving medication, and medication status
  was unavailable for 85.
- Those clinical categories are source variables with substantial
  missingness. They do not establish treatment effects, uniform diagnostic
  interviews, current DSM criteria, remission, illness duration, or causal
  contrasts.
- The project entered its unrestricted sharing phase on 2020-01-01. Current
  public instructions point to Psychological Science Data Bank DOI
  `10.57760/sciencedb.o00115.00013`, then require a signed DUA to obtain the
  unzip password and phenotypic information.
- The DUA requires an annual research-progress report, prohibits leaking data
  and phenotypic information to third parties, and requires the PNAS citation
  and consortium acknowledgement.
- DIRECT Phase II is a later, distinct resource with a separate deposit, DUA,
  cohorts, product state, and 2026 sharing phase. It must not be merged into
  this Phase I capability.
- The original PNAS result and later classification papers use retained
  subsets and specific pipelines. Their sample sizes, exclusions, metrics, and
  performance are not dataset inventory or evidence of diagnostic utility.
