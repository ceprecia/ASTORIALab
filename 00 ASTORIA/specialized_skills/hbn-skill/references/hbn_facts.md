# HBN facts that control the review (hbn-skill)

Read this reference when the skill or the user asks for it.

## HBN facts that control the review

Use these as dated orientation and recheck them at invocation:

- HBN is a community-referred, transdiagnostic, cross-sectional developmental
  psychopathology resource for ages 5 to 21. It is not a healthy-control
  cohort, an epidemiologic sample, or an autism-specific cohort.
- Recruitment is enriched for perceived mental-health or learning concerns.
  Program totals and recruitment targets are not released analytic counts.
- The current program page reports more than 8,000 children and families
  evaluated since 2015. That is not a downloadable release count.
- The official numbered-release state is Release 11.0. Its release notes were
  announced in 2024 for a 2022-11-23 cutoff. Current metadata must determine
  exact participant and file availability.
- Official Release 11 materials report 4,867 phenotypic datasets, 4,142 EEG or
  eye-tracking datasets, and 3,905 multimodal MRI datasets. These are
  modality-level records, not automatically unique people with complete
  linkage.
- MRI, EEG, eye-tracking, and minimal demographics have open routes, although
  some non-BIDS INDI access requires NITRC registration.
- Complete phenotypes require the current HBN Data Use Agreement signed by the
  principal investigator and an institutional official, followed by approved
  access. Current duration and procedures must be rechecked.
- HBN materials describe general distribution under CC BY 4.0, but a
  participant subset is restricted to
  CC BY-NC-SA. The participant-level `Commercial_Use` field controls the exact
  scope.
- Full phenotype, imaging, EEG, and eye-tracking releases do not have identical
  participant coverage or timing. A participant can have one modality without
  another.
- Current official guidance describes HBN as cross-sectional. A small number
  of repeated variables does not make it a longitudinal cohort.
- Recruitment and acquisition evolved across project phases and New York City
  locations. MRI scanner and sequence differences remain design concerns.
- MRI collection ended in April 2023. Actigraphy curation was still underway
  in the current FAQ, voice and video were not released, and biological sample
  access was limited to a subset through a separate repository.
- HBN evaluation is not a comprehensive autism assessment. Diagnostic fields
  can include degrees of certainty and require their exact dictionary and
  diagnostic-process documentation.

“Open” does not mean every participant permits commercial use, every modality
is linked, or full phenotype data are available without an agreement.
