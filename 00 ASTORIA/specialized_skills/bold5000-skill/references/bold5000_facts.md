## BOLD5000 facts that control the review

Use these as dated orientation and recheck them at invocation:

- BOLD5000 includes four Carnegie Mellon graduate-student participants, ages
  24 to 27 in the primary report. Three participants, CSI1 to CSI3, completed
  15 functional sessions plus one anatomical session.
- CSI4 completed nine functional sessions plus an anatomical session because
  of discomfort. Its 3,108 trials are not a complete 5,254-trial record.
- For complete participants, the design contains 5,254 stimulus trials and
  4,916 unique images: 4,803 presented once, 112 presented four times, and one
  presented three times.
- The 1,000 scene images used 250 categories largely inspired by SUN, but the
  images themselves were selected through Google Search. The other 3,916
  unique images came directly from COCO and ImageNet. Category inspiration
  must not be recorded as SUN image provenance, and image-level rights must
  be verified for every proposed use.
- Images appeared for 1 second followed by 9 seconds of fixation in a slow
  event-related design. Participants gave like, dislike, or neutral judgments.
- Eight of the 15 functional sessions included a functional localizer.
  Behavior, daily-intake questionnaires, cardiac, and respiratory records have
  product-specific availability.
- The study used one 3 T Siemens Verio scanner with a 32-channel head coil.
  It is a small, intensive, single-site, within-participant dataset, not a
  population cohort.
- KiltHub BOLD5000 Release 2.0, version 2 posted 2021-06-15, DOI
  `10.1184/R1/14456124`, is a 121.85 GB complete re-release of functional data
  with optimized GLM response estimation. Its item is marked CC0.
- OpenNeuro `ds001499` Release 1, currently version 1.3.1, and original
  Figshare products contain different raw, BIDS, behavioral, physiological,
  processed, or ROI assets. They are not identical to Release 2.0.
- The release-item license does not automatically resolve the copyright or
  redistribution terms of every SUN, COCO, or ImageNet stimulus. Verify the
  intended image-level use separately.

Do not treat four participants as four independent population replications,
5,254 trials as unique stimuli, or CSI4 as complete.
