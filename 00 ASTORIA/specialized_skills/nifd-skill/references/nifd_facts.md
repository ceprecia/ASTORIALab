# NIFD facts that control the review (nifd-skill)

Read this reference when the skill or the user asks for it.

## NIFD facts that control the review

Use these as dated orientation and recheck them at invocation:

- NIFD is the nickname for the Frontotemporal Lobar Degeneration
  Neuroimaging Initiative, FTLDNI, NIH grant `R01 AG032306`, coordinated
  through UCSF and disseminated by LONI IDA.
- NIFD is controlled access. The current route requires an IDA account,
  application, declared users and analyses, and acceptance of the NIFD DUA.
- The DUA prohibits reidentification, direct participant contact, and
  redistribution; binds the research team; requires institutional and IRB
  compliance, safeguards, breach reporting, prescribed acknowledgements, and
  current version and update checks.
- NIFD and the 4-Repeat Tauopathy Neuroimaging Initiative use the same imaging
  and clinical methods, and the official DUA says their controls are the same.
  Shared controls must not be counted as independent participants when
  datasets are combined.
- Current public documentation does not expose a complete live participant,
  visit, diagnosis, modality, or processed-product inventory before approval.
  Do not hard-code one.
- Processed data may be preliminary and may change as methods evolve. The DUA
  requires recording versions and rechecking updates before publication.
- A 2019 longitudinal analysis included structural MRI, DTI, ASL, clinical,
  and neuropsychological measures, but combined most FTLDNI participants with
  a separate UCSF `AG019724` study.
- That analysis's 161 patients and 137 controls are analysis-specific counts,
  not current NIFD archive counts.
- In that paper, structural MRI was pooled across UCSF, Massachusetts General
  Hospital, and Mayo scanners, while DTI and ASL analyses were restricted to
  UCSF because multisite methods were less established. This is paper context,
  not proof of current archive completeness.
- The old UCSF NIFD link now routes to the successor ALLFTD study. ALLFTD is
  not the current NIFD archive inventory.
- OpenNeuro `ds004403` is not a usable public NIFD source. The official
  OpenNeuro state reports that the dataset was deleted as a duplicate, and no
  live DataCite DOI establishes a current CC0 snapshot.

Do not call NIFD public, CC0, BIDS, single-scanner, or completely multimodal
without exact current archive evidence.
