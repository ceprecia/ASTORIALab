## HCP-YA facts that control the review

Use these as dated orientation and recheck them at invocation:

- The current official release is the HCP Young Adult 2025 Release, dated
  2025-08-11, distributed through ConnectomeDB powered by BALSA.
- Official documentation explicitly warns against mixing data from the 2025
  release with S1200 because the processing and release states differ.
- The 2025 release reports minimally processed or updated processed data for
  1,071 participants who had no processing errors and at least one
  resting-state fMRI run usable for MSMAll. This is a processed-release
  eligibility count, not the total cohort count.
- The same release reports unprocessed imaging for 1,113 participants and
  non-imaging data for 1,206 participants. These are different grains and
  must not be treated as one complete-case sample.
- The separate HCP Retest project contains 46 retested participants with
  second-visit data. The 2025 processed release reports 45 retest participants
  meeting its processing criteria. The 46-person retest cohort and the
  45-person processed state are different grains, and retest observations are
  not independent new participants.
- Seven-tesla and MEG data are subset resources, not universal coverage.
- HCP-YA includes structural MRI, resting-state fMRI, task fMRI, diffusion
  MRI, extensive non-imaging measures, and selected 7T and MEG data.
- The seven task-fMRI families are MOTOR, EMOTION, GAMBLING, LANGUAGE,
  RELATIONAL, SOCIAL, and WORKING MEMORY. Availability, run completeness,
  conditions, contrasts, and behavioral outputs must be verified for the
  selected package and participant set.
- HCP-YA deliberately includes twins and other siblings. Family structure and
  repeated observations must be represented in design, power, and analysis
  planning rather than removed by an invented default.
- Open-access and restricted data remain distinct. Restricted information
  requires the current eligibility, agreement, security, use, and
  dissemination conditions.
- A release-level modality list does not establish participant-level or
  run-level completeness.

Do not treat the processed 1,071, unprocessed 1,113, non-imaging 1,206,
46-person retest cohort, 45-person processed-retest state, 7T subset, or MEG
subset as interchangeable sample counts.
