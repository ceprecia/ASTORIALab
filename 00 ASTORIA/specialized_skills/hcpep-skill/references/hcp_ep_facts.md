## HCP-EP facts that control the review

Use these as dated orientation and recheck them at invocation:

- The current listed release is HCP-EP Release 1.1, released 2021-08-19
  through NDA Collection 2914.
- The release page and main manual summarize clinical and behavioral data for
  251 participants: 57 affective psychosis, 126 non-affective psychosis, and
  68 controls.
- A separate official release-detail page says 252 participants. Appendix 3
  reports 57, 127, and 68, which sum to 252, while its site table totals 251.
  This is an unresolved official-source conflict.
- Imaging is reported for 183 participants. Minimally preprocessed structural
  imaging is reported for 169. Neither count equals the full clinical and
  behavioral sample.
- The 183-person imaging cohort is reported as 34 affective psychosis,
  91 non-affective psychosis, and 58 controls.
- Released imaging includes T1-weighted and T2-weighted structural MRI,
  resting-state fMRI, diffusion MRI, and field maps. No task fMRI is listed.
- Release 1.1 is baseline only. Longitudinal follow-up belongs to separate NDA
  Collection 3179 and must not be inferred from the baseline release.
- Four recruitment sites contributed participants. MRI acquisition occurred
  on three Siemens Prisma scanners at Brigham and Women’s Hospital, McLean
  Hospital, and Indiana University. Coil configurations differed.
- Imaging, demographic, and behavioral assessments can occur months apart, so
  their recorded ages and clinical states can differ.
- Affective psychosis includes more than bipolar psychosis, and non-affective
  psychosis includes more than schizophrenia. Exact diagnosis codes and
  criteria control the selected release.
- Current access is NDA-controlled through institutional sponsorship and a
  Data Use Certification. Exact current eligibility, FWA, renewal, security,
  and sharing rules must be rechecked.

“Early psychosis” does not remove medication, chronicity, recruitment-site,
diagnostic, or assessment-timing heterogeneity.
