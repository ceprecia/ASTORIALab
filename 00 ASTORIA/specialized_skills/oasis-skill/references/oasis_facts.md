# OASIS facts that control the review (oasis-skill)

Read this reference when the skill or the user asks for it.

## OASIS facts that control the review

Use these as dated orientation and recheck them at invocation:

- OASIS-1 is a cross-sectional collection of 416 participants aged 18 to 96.
  It includes three or four T1-weighted acquisitions in one session per
  participant, 100 participants over age 60 with very mild to moderate
  Alzheimer disease, and a separate reliability sample of 20 nondemented
  participants scanned again within 90 days.
- OASIS-2 is a longitudinal collection of 150 participants aged 60 to 96
  across 373 imaging sessions. Participants have at least two visits separated
  by one year or more. It contains 72 participants characterized as
  nondemented throughout, 64 characterized as demented at entry and later
  visits, and 14 characterized as nondemented at entry and demented later.
- OASIS-3 is a retrospective compilation across several Knight ADRC projects
  and about 30 years. Its current project page contains three incompatible
  person counts: a 1,378-participant summary, a 1,379-subject counter, and
  displayed groups of 755 cognitively normal plus 622 with cognitive decline,
  which sum to 1,377. The same summary reports 2,842 MR sessions. Preserve all
  three person counts as an unresolved current-source conflict. The original
  release paper described 1,098 participants. These are source-state grains,
  not interchangeable sample counts.
- Current OASIS-3 documentation lists T1w, T2w, FLAIR, ASL, SWI, time-of-flight
  angiography, resting-state BOLD, and diffusion imaging, plus FreeSurfer
  products for many but not all MR sessions.
- OASIS-3 also includes PIB, AV45, and FDG PET plus PUP products. The separate
  OASIS-3 AV1451 project contains 451 tau-PET sessions and requires its own
  access request.
- OASIS-4 is a distinct clinical cohort, not an OASIS-3 update. Current source
  documentation describes 663 participants aged 21 to 94 who presented with
  memory complaints and 676 MR sessions.
- OASIS advises against combining OASIS-1, OASIS-2, and OASIS-3. Participants
  can occur in more than one release under different identifiers. OASIS-4 may
  be combinable with another release only after exact official linkage,
  identity, consent, and design conditions are verified.
- OASIS-3 dates are normalized to days from study entry. Imaging and clinical
  visits do not become paired merely because their day labels are close. The user
  must approve a defensible matching rule and sensitivity analysis.
- Clinical Dementia Rating, neuropsychological measures, diagnoses, imaging,
  biomarkers, and processed products have release-specific availability and
  timing. One measure must not silently stand in for a diagnosis or disease
  mechanism.
- OASIS-1 and OASIS-2 are predominantly 1.5 T T1-weighted collections.
  OASIS-3 is a retrospective multimodal compilation and must not inherit one
  scanner or protocol description from the earlier releases.
- The July 2025 Data Use Agreement limits OASIS data to noncommercial academic
  research absent prior written authorization. It requires secure,
  access-controlled storage; access only by the authorized user; no sharing
  with collaborators unless they independently obtain access; immediate
  breach reporting and mitigation; and secure destruction or return when the
  approved work ends or OASIS requests it.
- The same agreement prohibits reidentification, contact, facial recognition,
  and face-reconstructable derivatives. It contains no explicit AI or model
  clause. Public-AI upload is still incompatible with its authorized-user,
  no-further-disclosure, and security terms.
- Proposed publications or presentations using AV45 or AV1451 PET require
  Avid review and comment 30 days in advance.
- The live project page currently routes datasets through NITRC-IR, while the
  July 2025 agreement still mentions Central XNAT for OASIS-3 and OASIS-4.
  Preserve the dated conflict and verify the actual approved route.
