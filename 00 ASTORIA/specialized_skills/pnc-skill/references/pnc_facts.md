# PNC facts that control the review (pnc-skill)

Read this reference when the skill or the user asks for it.

## PNC facts that control the review

Use these as dated orientation and recheck them at invocation:

- The current official dbGaP accession is `phs000607.v3.p2`.
- The current dbGaP page reports 9,496 consented participants and more than
  9,700 MRI images. Images are not participants, and neither figure is the
  exact usable imaging-subject count.
- The official Penn project page describes more than 9,500 youths ages 8 to 21
  and an original imaging subsample of 1,445. The selected release manifest and
  modality completeness determine the actual analytic candidate set.
- The sample came through the Greater Philadelphia Children’s Hospital of
  Philadelphia health network, not psychiatric-service recruitment.
- The design includes clinical interview, the Penn Computerized
  Neurocognitive Battery, genetics, and multimodal neuroimaging.
- Verified imaging includes T1-weighted structural MRI, diffusion MRI, ASL,
  resting-state fMRI, fractal N-back spatial working-memory fMRI, and
  face-emotion-identification fMRI.
- Behavioral Penn CNB measures such as line orientation or word memory are not
  automatically fMRI tasks.
- Individual-level data use dbGaP Authorized Access and the study-specific Data
  Use Certification, not the NIMH Data Archive.
- dbGaP exposes public summaries and variable descriptions, while individual
  phenotype, imaging, and genetic records require authorization.
- dbGaP labels the study prospective and longitudinal, but longitudinal imaging
  cannot be assumed without exact repeat-visit evidence.
- Official Penn and dbGaP materials conflict on a 32-channel versus 12-channel
  head coil. Exact acquisition metadata controls the selected records.
- Historical screening and diagnostic information can use DSM-IV-era
  constructs and must not be flattened into a definitive current clinical
  diagnosis.
- OpenNeuro `ds000030` is the UCLA Consortium for Neuropsychiatric Phenomics
  dataset, not a PNC subset.

Do not use a file or image count as a participant count.
