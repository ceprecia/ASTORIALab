# Plan Publication

Match one research output to a suitable publication route and current requirements.

## Purpose

Compare candidate venues or establish the requirements for one selected
journal, article type and publication stage. Distinguish fit, external rules
and the user's choices. Return requirements that writers and package
preparers can use without taking ownership of manuscript prose.

## Inputs

- Publication objective, intended audience, research output and current version.
- Supported contribution, its provisional status, study type and known limits.
- Candidate or selected venues and the stage: initial submission, revision,
  accepted manuscript, preprint or another explicitly named route.
- Relevant funder/institution, access preferences, timing and known cost limits.
- Invoking session, return owner and decisions already supplied.

## Steps

1. Bound the comparison or requirements question. Use an existing abstract,
   outline or result summary to identify scope; do not invent missing findings.
   Separate a venue suggestion from a venue the user has selected.
2. Read [current primary-source verification](../research-output-coordinator/references/source_verification.md)
   completely and apply it to this task. Identify the controlling official
   venue pages and any task-specific editorial instructions.
3. For each candidate, inspect its scope, accepted article types, readership
   and relevant published work. Explain the fit using the actual research
   object; a journal title, metric or publisher reputation alone is insufficient.
4. Verify applicable author instructions, review route, access/licensing
   options, current fees and relevant funder or institutional constraints.
   Mark an institutional agreement or fee eligibility unconfirmed when the
   necessary institution-specific evidence is unavailable.
5. Compare only criteria relevant to the user: scope, audience, article type,
   access, costs, required materials and stated timing. Label unavailable
   information and distinguish published service statistics from a forecast
   for this manuscript. Do not promise review speed or acceptance.
6. For a selected destination, map requirements to the exact article type and
   stage. Include relevant limits, required sections, files, declarations,
   reporting standards, figures, supplementary materials and availability
   requirements, with conditions that determine whether each applies.
7. Check any proposed preprint or parallel dissemination route against the
   selected publisher's current policy. Preserve the distinction between
   scholarly sharing and media release; do not infer one universal embargo.
8. Surface material conflicts and the smallest unresolved decision. Continue
   comparisons or unaffected requirements work when one policy is inaccessible.
   If several viable choices remain, present their tradeoffs for user selection.
9. Return the bounded plan and source state to the original owner. Identify
   the selected next task and its owner without launching unrequested work.

## Outputs

Return a concise venue comparison or a selected-target requirements map,
depending on the request. Include:

- Exact target, article type, stage and inspected manuscript version.
- Evidence for fit; relevant requirements and the shared contract's source state.
- Costs, eligibility or timing that still need confirmation.
- User decisions, remaining alternatives and the next useful action.

Return the result in the current task's requested form or under the recipient's existing reporting policy. Follow me.md for an authorized saved artifact. Reuse the owning project's context rather than creating another hub.

## Boundaries

- The user selects the venue, scientific contribution and publication strategy.
- Official rules constrain the destination; they do not establish scientific
  validity or authorize changing the study to improve apparent journal fit.
- Requirements work does not submit a manuscript, contact a journal, accept
  an agreement or establish permission to distribute an output.
- University advice and example papers inform planning; use the selected
  publisher's current applicable instructions for its requirements.
- A requirements map is scoped to the inspected target and stage. It is not
  a standing certification for later submissions or another article type.

## Handoffs

- Preserve the invoking session and return owner throughout.
- Send selected requirements and source locations to
  [prepare-publication-package](../prepare-publication-package/SKILL.md).
- Borrow shared writing skills for requested prose and
  [check-research-reporting](../check-research-reporting/SKILL.md)
  for a bounded reporting audit.
- Return scientific framing choices to the research lead; use
  [research-critique](../research-critique/SKILL.md) for requested
  scientific critique, and Research Grant Advisor for application requirements.

## Checks

- Every material requirement has an exact target, stage and supporting source.
- The comparison describes the supplied research rather than generic prestige.
- Conditional requirements and institution-specific unknowns remain visible.
- Recommendations, official rules and user selections are distinguishable.
- The recipient can identify what to prepare next and what remains undecided.
