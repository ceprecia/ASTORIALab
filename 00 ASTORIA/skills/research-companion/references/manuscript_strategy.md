# Manuscript strategy

Read this reference for scientific contribution framing, a requested writing
roadmap, or domain review of returned manuscript text. Research Companion owns
scientific framing and synthesis. Shared writing assistance owns argument
planning, section drafting, independent writing reviews and integration through
writing-companion and its internal writing-coordinator.

## Scientific frame

Use when the study's central contribution, figure story or scientific emphasis
is unclear. Return only what the current task needs:

1. The larger question and current central claim, explicitly adopted,
   provisional or unresolved.
2. The supplied results or evidence that support it, with claim boundaries.
3. If useful, two or three defensible contribution options and the strongest
   contrary case. If only one is defensible, do not invent alternatives.
4. Genuine unanswered scientific decisions, their consequences and the smallest
   question needed from the user.
5. A bounded handoff to the shared writing team.

Do not replace a supplied construct, condition or outcome with a convenient
near-synonym. Preserve current study terminology, distinctions and rejected
formulations. A recommendation is not adoption. A supplied frame can be developed
without comparing it again; provisional aims can be outlined without pretending
that the gap, hypothesis or scope is settled.

Research Companion supplies scientific decisions and options rather than
candidate manuscript sentences. Content-specific writers produce the text.
Reporting-field completeness belongs to check-research-reporting; a scientific
critique belongs to research-critique. Citation identity/support, specificity,
paragraph reasoning and template fidelity have separate writing reviewers.

## Working roadmap

Use a task-sized roadmap when the user asks for planning or when a material
scientific choice needs an explicit comparison. Reuse an existing selected
contribution without another confirmation. Do not impose an additional roadmap
approval before a requested bounded draft.

Include the section purpose, current claim/status, needed argumentative moves,
minimum evidence, links back to the central argument, current structure and
material unknowns. For a Discussion, connect the study answer to what it changes
in field understanding and why that matters. For a Conclusion, carry forward the
same supported contribution rather than introducing a new one.

The shared argument planner can help select an outline or passage plan suited to the requested document. Use a named paragraph framework only when the recipient selects it. Preserve source support, inferential limits and the purpose of each passage; neither a bibliography nor an analysis inventory establishes the document's argument.

Current user direction and verified evidence govern. A roadmap guides emphasis
and sequence; it is not a second scientific authority. Rhetorical adaptation,
paragraph reordering, clarification and synthesis do not reopen adopted choices.
Ask about a scientific contradiction or missing decision only for the affected
claim, while continuing supported or explicitly provisional work.

Return roadmaps in the current task's requested form or under the recipient's existing reporting policy. Apply Research Companion's save authorization rule to a roadmap. Existing planning records are
context unless the user chooses to reuse their contents.

## Writing handoff

Read `00 ASTORIA/skills/writing-coordinator/references/writing_contract.md` and
`00 ASTORIA/skills/writing-coordinator/references/cross_system_handoff.md` for the
shared brief and return. The original caller remains the visible session owner.

Pass the exact draft/version and bounded section, the central argument/status,
section purpose, user-selected structure, current terminology, frozen material,
source locations, analysis or methods records, claim limits and open decisions.
Include the task-relevant MOC writing instructions and any recipient-supplied writing guidance. Workers read those sources; a skill name alone is not a load.

Use writing-companion for coordinated writing support, or the exact installed
content-specific writer for one bounded section. The internal writing-coordinator
assembles only needed argument/evidence, citation, structure, substance,
argument-reasoning, translation and integration roles. Multiple section writers may work
independently, with one integrate-writing owner for the canonical document.

Technical facts return through research-coordinator and the appropriate existing
analysis, modality, method or adapter specialist. Ask for actual input identities,
procedures, settings, outputs and status, not remembered software defaults. A
translation worker can turn the specialist record into methods-ready material;
the technical owner checks that account against the actual work. Preserve
planned, executed, technically checked, visually reviewed and user-accepted
states separately. Writing support does not authorize new analyses or acceptance.

## Returned prose review

Research Companion performs the domain fidelity review after writing-team
checks. Compare the returned section with current instructions, verified sources,
scientific choices and the section's purpose. Review every supplied variant
independently. Surface material scientific conflicts and unanswered choices.
Return a concrete defect to its responsible owner within the existing task scope.

Check that:

- The controlling scientific claim is supported and its provisional status has
  survived where applicable.
- Constructs, conditions, outcomes, comparisons and estimands preserve their
  supplied meanings. An altered label cannot silently change the science.
- Direct findings, interpretation, speculation and planned work remain distinct.
- Sources support the propositions attributed to them. A bibliography entry or
  verified DOI alone does not establish proposition support.
- Technical detail is sufficient for the requested account: design, measurement,
  procedure, analysis inputs/targets/comparisons and decision criteria are stated
  when supported; missing details remain explicit rather than invented.
- argument-reasoning findings are resolved substantively. Labels, connecting words and
  smooth prose do not demonstrate that evidence warrants the claim.

For a Discussion, check the larger answer, field advance and stakes; the sequence
should develop interpretation rather than reconstruct an analysis/region/figure
inventory. Every retained finding needs a conceptual, theoretical or methodological
consequence supported by evidence. Literature relationships should specify whether
the work confirms, extends, refines, reconciles, challenges or constrains earlier
work, with the reason. Preregistered outcomes remain accurately reported but do
not mechanically determine paragraph order. Retain limitations that materially
bound the contribution, with a targeted unresolved question rather than an apology
inventory. Do not demand that substantive quantitative detail disappear merely
to pass a rhetorical test.

For a Conclusion, verify that it carries forward the same supported answer and
stakes, adds no new evidence or stronger claim, and closes on an earned implication.

Independent writing reviewers own their detailed checks. The companion does not
redraft the passage or collapse their jobs into a generic approval. Return the
requested supported draft with any material remaining limitations; do not imply
that stylistic quality, a complete heading list or a reviewer pass settles science.
