# Research strategy workflows

Use this reference before Research Companion performs Explore and Frame,
Strategy and Triage, or a conditional strategy micro-check. Routing alone does
not require this reference. Use the user's current scope and the companion's
core evidence and human-control rules.

## Working modes

Use the modes needed for the task; ask about a mode only when it changes an unresolved material choice.

### Explore and Frame

Use when the user wants to compare or develop a bounded set of frames, directions,
scopes, cross-field connections, or possible contributions. The input may be
The user's seed or a supplied Scientific Brainstorming idea brief.

If the user wants to widen the option space through a named ideation heuristic,
technique-guided divergence, or heuristic connection mapping before strategic comparison, hand off to
[scientific-brainstorming](../../scientific-brainstorming/SKILL.md).
If the requested connection rests on primitives, equations, algorithms, or
source-to-target mathematical correspondence, use
[first-principles-research](../../first-principles-research/SKILL.md)
through Part 1 instead.

Possible outputs:

- two to four research frames
- alternative scopes or angles
- a central problem and gap map
- cross-field connections with reasons the connection may or may not hold
- an ASCII mindmap
- assumptions and open questions
- a smallest useful exploratory next step

Develop the user's seed using the proposal and clarification rules in Steps 2–4; detailed design and method work retain specialist owners.

### Strategy and Triage

Use when the user wants to compare directions, evaluate whether a project remains worth pursuing, identify opportunity costs, or decide among continue, refine, pause, and stop options.

Possible outputs:

- strategic comparison table
- strongest argument for and against each option
- feasibility and resource analysis
- opportunity-cost analysis
- provisional recommendation and strongest contrary case
- a decision checkpoint for the user
- the smallest test that could reduce strategic uncertainty

Continue, refine, pause, and stop are labeled options, not an AI verdict. The user makes the decision.

## Conditional strategy micro-checks

Use these as compact internal reasoning checks inside the existing working
mode. Do not turn them into new skills, parts, mandatory output sections,
literature searches, analyses, or independent approval gates. Surface only the
parts that materially clarify the current decision or frame. The Discussion
exception below makes the supported result of the Gap-to-Contribution Trace
visible inside the existing Research Writing Roadmap. It creates no separate
artifact or second confirmation.

### Gap-to-Contribution Trace

Activate this trace only when the user is framing a project, manuscript
contribution, or central argument:

`established terrain or tension -> supported gap, limitation, or unresolved problem -> why it matters -> important and addressable focus -> proposed or user-supplied question -> objective -> strongest calibrated contribution`

- Ground the terrain and gap in supplied or verified evidence. Label an
  unverified gap provisional rather than manufacturing novelty.
- Label proposed questions and distinguish them from the user's supplied or
  adopted question; an interesting gap is not adoption.
- Distinguish the objective from the contribution. A study activity is not
  itself a contribution, and an unaddressable gap does not become important
  merely because it exists.
- Treat this as a planning view, not an Introduction template, citation order,
  completeness checklist, or replacement for an approved manuscript
  structure.

For every new Discussion roadmap or major Discussion reframing, run this
meaning trace before selecting results or paragraph topics:

`Introduction big question -> supported study answer -> field advance -> why it matters -> minimum evidence -> relationship to prior work -> remaining uncertainty`

- Express the field advance with an accurate relationship such as `confirms`,
  `extends`, `refines`, `reconciles`, `challenges`, or `constrains`.
- Present two or three materially distinct contribution emphases when the
  supplied and verified evidence supports them. Each option must state the
  study answer, field advance, stakes, minimum evidence, and claim boundary.
- Do not manufacture a second option. If only one contribution survives the
  evidence boundary, show that option and explain briefly why no second
  scientifically defensible emphasis was found.
- Treat result details, analysis names, brain-region inventories,
  preregistered outcomes, and citations as support for the contribution. Do
  not let them define the contribution or its order.

### Uncertainty-Priority Loop

Activate this loop only when at least two material uncertainties compete, or
when one uncertainty could change the current decision, claim ceiling,
required disclosure, or route:

`decision or inference -> uncertainty -> consequence if wrong -> information needed -> effort, urgency, and reversibility -> provisional priority -> update or reverse-if condition`

- Apply the existing source precedence and the user's current decisions before
  ranking an uncertainty. Technical uncertainty does not outrank a current
  collaborator request or a verified contradiction automatically.
- Identify the smallest informative check, but do not search, analyze, execute,
  save, or invoke another capability without the permission and route that
  action already requires.
- Stop the loop when the remaining uncertainty would not change the current
  decision, claim ceiling, required disclosure, or route. Record residual
  interpretation or future-work uncertainty without creating a present task.
- Do not rerun the loop recursively unless new evidence or a changed constraint
  reaches the recorded update or reverse-if condition.
