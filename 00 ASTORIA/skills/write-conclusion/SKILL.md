# Write Conclusion

Close with the answer the work earned and the question it usefully leaves open.

## Purpose

Draft or revise a standalone Conclusion or closing research passage from an
established argument. Distill the answer, contribution and significance without
repeating a Results inventory, reintroducing the field, or strengthening the
science in the last paragraph.

## Inputs

Read the [writing contract](../writing-coordinator/references/writing_contract.md)
completely. Use the [cross-system handoff](../writing-coordinator/references/cross_system_handoff.md)
when domain-specialist material or requirements are involved.

Read the relevant MOC by the default in `me.md`, then the sections needed for
this assignment. Read any recipient-supplied writing guidance identified in the brief. If none is supplied, use the document-purpose and evidence requirements and the actual task sources. Writing preferences are not evidence for the project's scientific claims.

Recover the current writing brief: exact document/version, audience, section
purpose, central argument and its provisional or adopted status, any recipient-selected organizational requirements,
source locators, protected terminology, claim ceiling, required structure and
length, and authorized edits. Apply only a writing profile selected for this task or in the recipient's `me.md`. The available references include `writing-style-positive-scientific` and `writing-style-negative-ai`. Apply each independently when selected. Ask only for missing information that changes the
requested wording; continue unaffected work without inventing an answer.

Read the current research question, Discussion or equivalent argument, and
findings that establish its answer. Recover the accepted contribution, its
limits and any explicitly requested future direction. If the user requests a
provisional scaffold, retain the provisional status of the contribution.

## Steps

1. State internally the question, supported answer, contribution and warranted
   consequence already established in the document. Distinguish them from
   aspiration, speculation or an unselected interpretation. A Conclusion does
   not itself require a new contribution-choice packet or roadmap approval.
2. Identify the one or few messages the closing must retain. Do not merely
   collect each section's last sentence or every significant result. Include
   enough result language to identify the basis of the answer and its scope.
3. Draft a closing passage in the requested form. Carry forward only the established answer and justified consequence, with enough context to make its scope clear. Do not require a fixed sequence of rhetorical components or add an unsupported ending.
4. Keep the contribution at the level actually established. Preserve material
   boundaries on population, task, measurement, inference or application.
   Do not turn a narrow result into a general mechanism, treatment benefit,
   technical qualification or promise of future success.
5. Add a future question/direction only when it follows from the remaining
   uncertainty and the brief calls for it. Explain what it could clarify rather
   than ending with a generic demand for more research.
6. Match terminology and inference strength to the current body. Check any
   new noun, comparison, citation or implication against its earlier basis.
   If closing prose seems to need a new scientific premise, return that issue
   to the user/domain lead instead of introducing it here.
7. Remove redundant background, repeated thesis sentences and a list of values
   already reported elsewhere. Preserve a clear answer and its justified
   consequence. Check the required length and structure.

## Outputs

Return the closing passage first, followed only by material unresolved choices.
A requested provisional conclusion must be identified as provisional. Keep the
existing document owner and do not rewrite the Discussion to justify a stronger
closing claim unless that revision is also authorized.

## Boundaries

- Do not introduce new results or scientific claims absent from the body.
- Do not erase a limitation that determines the answer's meaning.
- Do not mistake a confident cadence for an evidence-supported contribution.
- If the central interpretation is unresolved, ask the smallest necessary
  question or provide the explicitly requested provisional scaffold.

## Handoffs

Return to the invoking writing owner. Send missing interpretive choices to the
user/domain lead. The integration editor checks consistency with the title,
abstract, Introduction and Discussion; independent reviewers check reasoning,
substance and source support as assigned.

## Checks

- Does the conclusion answer the question posed by the document?
- Is its contribution the same one established in the Discussion/body?
- Is significance earned by the stated evidence and reasoning?
- Does it preserve material scope and provisional status?
- Does the final sentence add a useful consequence or question rather than filler?
