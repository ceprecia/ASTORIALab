# Shared writing contract

## Required context

This contract governs document writing and review. Capture, transcripts, redaction, chat preparation, protocols and profile building retain their exact specialist workflows. Membership in Writing System does not require loading Writing Companion, Writing Coordinator or a manuscript brief for every task.

Every assigned worker reads its complete skill and applicable instructions. Reuse complete context already available and scale further reading to the requested job.

- A spelling or other routine local correction needs the supplied passage, requested operation and protected wording.
- A bounded specialist task needs its exact passage or artifact, purpose, source records, relevant definitions, constraints and permitted action. Read MOC or writing guidance when it supplies needed context, following the MOC reading policy in `me.md`. An unavailable whole-document argument does not prevent a supported procedural, descriptive or verification task.
- Coordinated substantive document development needs relevant project guidance, current argument and status, selected structure and any recipient-supplied writing guidance. Read available guidance once per context and pass the applicable parts to workers. When no personal guidance is supplied, use the document's purpose, applicable reporting requirements and evidence, leaving personal tastes unspecified. Each worker still reads the original sources needed for its assignment.

A missing source is a disclosed gap. Ask only when it materially affects the requested result and continue independent supported work. Do not reconstruct personal preferences or scientific facts from missing context.

Apply profiles as directed by `me.md` only to the prose being produced or revised. Apply the profiles selected for this task or explicitly recorded in the recipient's preferences. An explicit named-profile request may cover a derived summary while its transcript remains unchanged. Source records, quotations, captures and verification-only findings do not acquire a style-editing task merely through this system.

Recipient-supplied writing guidance may define personal preferences alongside the document-purpose and evidence requirements. Empirical statements in that guidance are not automatically verified evidence for a new manuscript. Use primary sources before reusing those claims as scientific support.

## Central argument and shared brief

For argument-bearing assignments, carry the controlling question, relevant claim and status. For other assignments, carry the actual procedural, descriptive or review purpose. Preserve the author's exact terminology and source-linked accepted choices. A claim can be **selected**, **working/provisional**, or **unresolved**. Do not convert a provisional objective, proposed aim or expected outcome into an established hypothesis or result.

The caller or active coordinator supplies the applicable parts of a compact brief identifying current artifact/revision, audience, section purpose, argument/status, chosen template, writing guidance, source locators, known facts, unknowns, frozen content, claim ceiling, authorized output and return owner. A clear direct request may already provide the brief. Workers check the relevant original sources rather than relying solely on a coordinator's paraphrase. A changed central argument, measure, comparison or template propagates to affected active assignments before integration.

## Argument and paragraph reasoning

Named paragraph frameworks and their specialist reviewers are optional recipient choices. Their presence in a catalog, dependency list or team example is availability, not a requirement to use that method.

Choose an organization that fits the document's purpose and the recipient's selected structure. Check that material claims have evidence, that the reasoning connecting them is stated where needed, and that sections contribute to the document's argument. Procedures, result descriptions and figure keys need the form appropriate to their function. A named paragraph framework is optional and used only when selected by the recipient.

## Substance before fluency

Write the concrete entity, operation, measure, comparison, observation and consequence relevant to the passage. Ask what a knowledgeable reader could identify, reproduce, evaluate or disagree with after reading it. Phrases such as “a shared bias,” “predict behavior,” or “a rigorous pipeline” are unfinished when the needed mechanism, target, comparison or operation is not specified.

Separate **contradicted**, **unsupported by supplied sources**, **underspecified**, **unverified**, and **explicitly provisional**. Do not label every vague sentence a hallucination. Do not invent a plausible sample size, circuit value, software flag, analysis target or causal explanation to make a passage specific. Return a precise missing-input question or a visibly incomplete scaffold and continue supported work.

Preserve distinctions among constructs, conditions, measurements and model outputs. A stylistic synonym can change the science. Claims about generalization name source and target conditions, training/test separation, outcome and comparison when these are needed for the intended claim. A technical execution record, QC review, physical measurement and adopted design are different evidence states.

Preserve entity-to-result relationships during compression. Distinguish one architecture from its separately tested variants, and one intervention acting on multiple targets from different interventions each acting on its own target. A generic singular can create an unsupported capability even when every noun and citation is accurate. Retain the relevant pairing or use explicit plural/distributive wording without inventing counts.

## Sources and citations

Trace material factual propositions to the actual supplied source or verified external source. Bibliographic existence, source access, and support for a particular proposition are separate checks. A DOI that resolves does not prove that the adjacent claim is supported. Abstract-only access supports only what the abstract establishes. Never reconstruct a reference, quotation, result or source location from familiarity.

Preserve numerical values, units, statistics, labels, source identities and uncertainty. If a source conflicts with current prose, return the exact discrepancy to the owner. A source can also conflict with itself: inspect the relevant alternate description, table or footnote before reporting a definitive parameter or declaring a contradiction. Retain both locators when the conflict remains unresolved. Keep reported observations, model demonstrations and author interpretations distinct; publication does not make a claim an unrestricted conclusion. Search when authorized/required by the task and tools; unavailable access remains visible. A verified citation does not validate causal inference or a scientific design.

## Drafting and review

Content-specific writers own their bounded prose. A direct bounded task uses its exact writer or reviewer without a compulsory team. For a substantive team draft, separate substance and argument reasoning reviewers from its authors. Add citation, structure, reporting and technical reviews when relevant. Reviewers return exact passage locators, evidence, the category of failure and the smallest repair or required input. A review can pass a supported limited claim without demanding unnecessary detail. Preserve review-only scope and unchanged prose when requested.

Source accuracy and reader understanding are separate checks. In a substance review, first read back the draft’s meaning at its stated audience and purpose without filling gaps from detailed source material. Then verify it against the sources. Define what a consequential metric measures before relying on its numeric value; preserve definitions and material conditions when shortening prose. A passing citation check does not cancel an unresolved substance or reasoning finding.

For rates, proportions and overlap scores, preserve what is counted and the reference set when these determine the inference. An accurate denominator with an undefined score can still leave the result unclear. Under a word limit, remove secondary detail before deleting the operation, decisive comparison or qualification. Judge the final shortened passage at its requested reading scope: context available in a full section may be absent from a standalone abstract or caption.

The integration editor owns the canonical mutable document. Other workers return isolated passages or findings. After integration, recheck changed facts, citations, structure and central-argument links; do not assume a pre-integration review covers altered text. The user's voice, current structure and frozen material survive polishing. Readability scores are optional diagnostics, never targets that justify deleting necessary terminology or evidence.

## Return and completion

Return directly to the invoking owner, through writing-coordinator only when it is already coordinating the task. Report the requested artifact, source material actually used, checks actually performed, important unresolved decisions and the next owner as needed. Drafted, source-checked, technically reviewed, author-accepted, formatted and submitted are distinct states. Do not call a section complete because its headings exist or its prose is fluent. Save only within the requested action and destination. Save agent workflow only when authorized, at the verified location selected for the task or established by the recipient's continuity policy. Preserve approved captures and study continuity.
