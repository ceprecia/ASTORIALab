# Shared writing contract

## Required context

Every writing worker reads its complete assigned skill, applicable project instructions, relevant MOC context and this contract. Read recipient-supplied writing guidance and current MOC writing guidance when available. If no personal writing guidance is supplied, use the document's purpose, applicable reporting requirements and evidence; leave personal writing tastes unspecified. A specifically requested but unavailable source remains a disclosed gap. Apply formal scientific profiles as directed by me.md only to prose production/revision.

Recipient-supplied writing guidance may define personal preferences alongside the document-purpose and evidence requirements. Empirical statements in that guidance are not automatically verified evidence for a new manuscript. Use primary sources before reusing those claims as scientific support.

## Central argument and shared brief

Carry the controlling question, central argument and status into every assignment. Preserve the author's exact terminology, source-linked accepted choices and the section's intended contribution. A claim can be **selected**, **working/provisional**, or **unresolved**. Do not convert a provisional objective, proposed aim or expected outcome into an established hypothesis or result.

The coordinator supplies a compact brief identifying current artifact/revision, audience, section purpose, argument/status, chosen template, current writing guidance, source locators, known facts, unknowns, frozen content, claim ceiling, authorized output and original return owner. Workers check the relevant original sources rather than relying solely on the coordinator's paraphrase. A changed central argument, measure, comparison or template propagates to affected active assignments before integration.

## Argument and paragraph reasoning

Named paragraph frameworks and their specialist reviewers are optional recipient choices. Their presence in a catalog, dependency list or team example is availability, not a requirement to use that method.

Choose an organization that fits the document's purpose and the recipient's selected structure. Check that material claims have evidence, that the reasoning connecting them is stated where needed, and that sections contribute to the document's argument. Procedures, result descriptions and figure keys need the form appropriate to their function. A named paragraph framework is optional and used only when selected by the recipient.

## Substance before fluency

Write the concrete entity, operation, measure, comparison, observation and consequence relevant to the passage. Ask what a knowledgeable reader could identify, reproduce, evaluate or disagree with after reading it. Phrases such as “a shared bias,” “predict behavior,” or “a rigorous pipeline” are unfinished when the needed mechanism, target, comparison or operation is not specified.

Separate **contradicted**, **unsupported by supplied sources**, **underspecified**, **unverified**, and **explicitly provisional**. Do not label every vague sentence a hallucination. Do not invent a plausible sample size, circuit value, software flag, analysis target or causal explanation to make a passage specific. Return a precise missing-input question or a visibly incomplete scaffold and continue supported work.

Preserve distinctions among constructs, conditions, measurements and model outputs. A stylistic synonym can change the science. Claims about generalization name source and target conditions, training/test separation, outcome and comparison when these are needed for the intended claim. A technical execution record, QC review, physical measurement and adopted design are different evidence states.

## Sources and citations

Trace material factual propositions to the actual supplied source or verified external source. Bibliographic existence, source access, and support for a particular proposition are separate checks. A DOI that resolves does not prove that the adjacent claim is supported. Abstract-only access supports only what the abstract establishes. Never reconstruct a reference, quotation, result or source location from familiarity.

Preserve numerical values, units, statistics, labels, source identities and uncertainty. If a source conflicts with current prose, return the exact discrepancy to the owner. A source can also conflict with itself: inspect the relevant alternate description, table or footnote before reporting a definitive parameter or declaring a contradiction. Retain both locators when the conflict remains unresolved. Keep reported observations, model demonstrations and author interpretations distinct; publication does not make a claim an unrestricted conclusion. Search when authorized/required by the task and tools; unavailable access remains visible. A verified citation does not validate causal inference or a scientific design.

## Drafting and review

Content-specific writers own their bounded prose. For a substantive team draft, separate substance and argument reasoning reviewers from its authors. Add citation, structure, reporting and technical reviews when relevant. Reviewers return exact passage locators, evidence, the category of failure and the smallest repair or required input, not generic instructions to improve clarity. A review can pass a supported limited claim without demanding unnecessary detail.

Source accuracy and reader understanding are separate checks. In a substance review, first read back the draft’s meaning at its stated audience and purpose without filling gaps from detailed source material. Then verify it against the sources. Define what a consequential metric measures before relying on its numeric value; preserve definitions and material conditions when shortening prose. A passing citation check does not cancel an unresolved substance or reasoning finding.

The integration editor owns the canonical mutable document. Other workers return isolated passages or findings. After integration, recheck changed facts, citations, structure and central-argument links; do not assume a pre-integration review covers altered text. The user's voice, current structure and frozen material survive polishing. Readability scores are optional diagnostics, never targets that justify deleting necessary terminology or evidence.

## Return and completion

Report the requested artifact, source material actually used, checks actually performed, important unresolved decisions and the next owner. Drafted, source-checked, technically reviewed, author-accepted, formatted and submitted are distinct states. Do not call a section complete because its headings exist or its prose is fluent. Keep readiness information in the existing owner and keep actions in the existing task system.
