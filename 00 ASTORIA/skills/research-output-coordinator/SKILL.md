# Research Output Coordinator

Select the bounded service that helps research outputs get checked, shared, published or preserved.

## Purpose

Own Part 5 capability discovery and routing for publication planning, output and
provenance verification, computational reproduction, release preparation and
verification, engagement, and closeout. Retain selected funding-application and
reporting routes. Research Companion keeps scientific framing and the visible
research session; Research Coordinator keeps team assignment and collection.
This catalog does not create a second writing team or a mandatory final phase.

## Inputs

Use the bounded requested outcome, original invoking/return owner, current
scientific frame and provisional decisions, exact artifact versions, available
sources, target destination/stage, authorization and expected completion evidence.
Read [current primary-source verification](references/source_verification.md)
for the selected task and the research-coordinator human-control contract when
scientific work is in scope. Reuse complete current-context reads.

## Steps

1. Identify the actual requested object and service. Use the routing table below;
   do not load the entire catalog or launch a fixed roster. A new contribution,
   interpretation or scientific choice returns to Research Companion and the
   user, with analysis/critique support as needed.
2. Verify the selected complete skill, its inputs and exact installed path.
   Give it the bounded sources/versions, scientific decisions, unresolved inputs,
   task authorization, owned output, source-verification requirement and original
   return owner. Preserve already-authorized follow-ups without repeating intake.
3. For policy-dependent advice, require current primary-source verification for
   the exact destination, material/article type and stage. A saved directory or
   previous requirement check is not a substitute. Repository tasks also read the
   [candidate reference](references/repositories.md) and inspect the owner policy.
4. For reproduction, use verify-computational-reproducibility for scope and
   comparison evidence; technical execution goes through analysis-coordinator
   and the exact selected technical owner. Preserve the original data, code,
   environment, manual workflow and user-selected comparison criteria.
5. Borrow shared writing through writing-companion/writing-coordinator or the
   exact requested content skill. Carry the current central argument/status,
   section purpose, selected structure, source locations, unknowns and relevant
   MOC/recipient-supplied writing guidance. Borrow check-research-reporting for disclosure
   coverage and artifact builders for their formats rather than duplicating them.
6. Collect the actual return and inspect the result/artifact within scope. Keep
   source currency, preparation, reporting completeness, execution, accepted QC,
   reproduced outputs, observed deposit state, public release and closeout
   separate. A document, successful command or DOI cannot certify another state.
7. Return the service result, source/comparison evidence, limitations and next
   action/owner. Reconcile only affected dependencies when a version or decision
   changes. Update existing project context only when authorized; progress logs
   use their capture owner. Report checks in the current task's requested form or under the recipient's existing reporting policy; a separate saved report requires authorization.
8. Continue independent requested work when one requirement or input remains
   inaccessible or unresolved. Follow me.md for external actions; preparation
   does not itself authorize submission, upload, public communication or release.

## Outputs

Return one bounded service result, its inspected versions, source state where
requirements matter, evidence of actual checks/execution, uninspected scope and
next owner/action. Keep useful authorized status in the existing MOC/action
tracker rather than creating another hub, registry or automatic audit record.

## Boundaries

- Coordinating a service does not choose scientific interpretations or criteria.
- A catalog entry is an available capability, not completed work or a team launch.
- Shared writing, scientific critique, technical execution and institutional
  commitments retain their existing owners and requirements.
- Deposit approval, accepted QC and reproduced results must be supported by their
  own evidence and cannot be inferred from completion of another service.

## Handoffs

Read `00 ASTORIA/skills/writing-coordinator/references/cross_system_handoff.md`
for writing assistance. Research Coordinator manages scoped workers and collection;
this coordinator selects the service and preserves the original session owner.
Grant Advisor retains its opportunity-specific authority rules and requirements
maintenance contract. Shared capture/protocol and artifact owners keep their
specialized work. No unrelated operation starts merely because one service ends.

For a requested final figure/table review, use `review-figures-tables` with the
actual exports, intended size and available `verify-research-outputs` return.
The reviewer sends practical presentation repairs to the builder or caption
writer and numerical discrepancies to the verifier. Recheck affected displays
after repair; preserve the original return owner without adding an approval step.

## Checks

- The selected skill exists, was read and receives a concrete bounded assignment.
- Current controlling sources and applicable stage are identified where needed.
- The reported completion state does not exceed inspected evidence.
- Technical and scientific ambiguities reach the appropriate owner.
- The return reaches the original owner without a second writing workflow.
