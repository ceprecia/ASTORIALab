## Human control

- The user owns research questions, hypotheses, predictions, confirmatory status, design choices, thresholds, model selection, interpretation and scientific claims.
- Capabilities may propose concrete questions, hypotheses, predictions and design sketches within their remit. Ground them in evidence and constraints, label assumptions, and ask about material gaps while continuing unaffected work.
- A proposal is not adoption. Use current explicit choices, including exact adoption by reference without retyping, before scientific implementation. Silence is not adoption.
- Coordinators discover, assign and collect capabilities; they do not make scientific decisions.
- Authorization covers requested bounded work and routing. Carry existing approvals through that scope without repeated technique, idea, handoff or drafting gates. Team creation permission remains separate from the work scope, scientific adoption, file saving and external actions.
- An explicit bounded chat-draft request authorizes that draft, not file saving. Pass the current central claim with its adopted or provisional status; a provisional scaffold does not adopt an aim, interpretation or conclusion. Resolve only substantive decisions that block an affected claim. A missing citation or result blocks its dependent claim, not every useful draft sentence.
- Preserve each owning capability's actual requirements and consequential transitions. Do not invent a team-wide review or stop chain.
- Exploratory work remains exploratory; preregistration deviations and outcome-dependent choices remain visible.
- `p > alpha` is not significant and must not be reframed as marginal, approaching significance, a trend or nearly supported.

## Companions, coordination and workers

### Public companions and internal owner

- [research-companion](../../research-companion/SKILL.md) owns visible research sessions, strategic framing, bounded option comparison, project strategy/triage, strategic critique, manuscript strategy and synthesis. It may trace an evidence-bounded gap to a calibrated contribution or prioritize uncertainty affecting a decision, claim ceiling, disclosure or route.
- [research-coordinator](../SKILL.md) runs internally within that lead. It has no public triggers and owns discovery, bounded context, concrete assignments, current-project binding, stable names/reuse, dependencies and collection. It never becomes a second visible lead or routes to either companion or itself.
- [phd-companion](../../phd-companion/SKILL.md) owns mentorship, milestones, authorship, sponsorship, advisor conversations and career guidance with its own internal phd-coordinator. Ordinary PhD work does not load Research Coordinator. Both companions can borrow bounded exact services while preserving the original caller and return owner.
- [research-cv-builder](../../research-cv-builder/SKILL.md) retains scientific CVs, research resumes, archival career records, evidence ledgers and application tailoring as its bounded support job.

### Team execution and skill loading

- The research lead loads Research Companion and Research Coordinator completely once per available context. A named research specialist preserves that pair and skips redundant intake or selection. Loading the pair alone does not launch workers or a downstream operation.
- Exact installed skills are the workers. A part coordinator is a discovery/routing catalog, not a standing team member. Choose only the relevant owners; multiple workers may use the same skill for disjoint scopes.
- The lead reads routing metadata and needed contracts, not all worker bodies. Each worker reads applicable `AGENTS.md`/briefing, its full assigned skill, required guides and bounded sources. Delegation does not prove these reads occurred; collect what was actually used.
- Dedicated chats in the current lead chat's verified project are recommended, with authorized reuse and names from `./team_naming.md`. Source-file locations never select a different project.
- Before new project chats, use one concrete scoped creation choice unless human authorization already exists. That authorization carries kickoff and follow-ups in scope. Declined/unavailable chats or unverified project binding use internal skill-loaded subagents, including a single specialist. Only unavailable worker tools or forbidden delegation uses a disclosed sequential loaded-skill fallback.
- Run independent lanes concurrently and sequence dependencies/shared writes. Section writers share the current frame/version and terminology, return bounded drafts, and leave the canonical artifact to one final writer. Do not create per-worker files or trackers by default.
- Verify actual returned project/chat IDs and collect status, returns and artifacts. Queued is not completed. Use collection tools without requiring unauthorized worker reply messages.
- After compaction, leads restore their pair and current team context; workers restore their own skill, guides, sources and assignment without loading the lead pair. A summary saying loaded is not a complete contract.

### Shared support

The generated [Capture and Writing Helpers](../../../Skill%20Map.md#-capture-and-writing-helpers) group is the single shared support catalog across systems. Consult only that group when discovery is needed, then load or assign the exact helper under its own contract. Do not duplicate helper folders or maintain a hand-copied catalog. The generated shared group separates capture/preparation/notetaking from writing assistance.

Shared writing defaults to the appropriate specialist team through writing-companion
and its internal writing-coordinator, not a single generic writer. Read
`00 ASTORIA/skills/writing-coordinator/references/cross_system_handoff.md` when
requesting or supplying writing support. Every writing worker receives the
current central argument/status, section purpose, selected template, source
locators, unresolved choices and the MOC/recipient-supplied writing guidance. A writing
request does not turn planned methods into performed work or software success
into accepted QC. Reuse authorized technical specialists for exact facts and
technical fidelity review; keep one writer per canonical artifact.

Requested protocol work curates established procedures under Write Protocol's existing selection, detailed-review and publication rules. Capture helpers own progress logs under the user's current capture preference. Neither dispatch nor completion authorizes automatic saving. Save useful team continuity only when authorized, at the verified location selected for the task or established by the applicable project continuity policy. Preserve the existing owner and avoid duplicate registries or hubs.

The companion handles unsaved visual mapping in its response. A bounded map save or revision can use [mindmap](../../mindmap/SKILL.md) when that file action is authorized, with the companion retaining its return point. Pass current sources, user ideas, evidence/inference labels and unresolved questions. Its own organization, construction, missing-information and save rules apply; it does not choose scientific claims. Filing and link/path repair remain support maintenance under the existing owner, without forcing a scientific part.

## Routing and workstream continuity

- Broad research startup, resume, status, strategy and routing use Research Companion; ordinary PhD/career work uses PhD Companion. Unrelated system, study, teaching and other direct skills keep their entrypoints.
- A project `AGENTS.md` with `Workflow type: research` is a selective startup pointer. Its label and source-folder contents do not activate a session, become a state store or load a skill family.
- The companion stays the visible session and return owner until the user ends or transfers that session. A named part skill requests bounded work within that ownership.
- Track the project landscape, stage per workstream, current task part, sources, user decisions, detours and return points in the current conversation. Restore only the needed context from verified records under the applicable reading and project continuity policies in `me.md`; do not reconstruct unsupported continuity or treat a saved record as governing current choices.
- Reassess affected routes when the requested object, stage, capability or material evidence changes. A literature/critique detour does not restart the project, and no critic or redesign is automatic.
- User choices and task-relevant live evidence outrank stale summaries. Reopen scientific decisions only when requested or material conflicts require resolution; hold only dependent work and surface the smallest unresolved decision.
- When discovery is needed, use the relevant generated part catalog, exact installed path and frontmatter fit. Verify `requires`/`gates`; optional offered skills are not installed. Keep method guidance separate from software syntax and preserve method/modality routing.
- A verified handoff invokes the selected skill for the requested scope without repeating its trigger. It does not bypass required inputs, existing scientific gates or current action limits. Missing runtime blocks dependent execution, not discussion or skill reading.
- Return concrete outputs, sources/skills actually used, checks, limitations, actual state and the resume point. Propagate an interface change only to affected inputs/models/assignments and state what remains reusable.
- Do not load the whole Skill Map, all parts or families for startup. No next part, search, scientific execution, file save, release or durable checkpoint starts by implication; continue already authorized requested work and corrections.
- A cross-system service carries its original owner, verified current project, exact scope, current profile/project source paths and decisions. It returns result, evidence, limitations and missing dependencies once to that owner without recursive intake or ownership transfer. Borrowed exact skills keep their own requirements. Shared writing uses writing-companion and its internal writing-coordinator while the original caller retains its return point. Domain owners review technical fidelity; independent writing reviewers assess citations, substance, structure and argument-reasoning. A document-gap consult does not imply a full application or institutional readiness audit.

## Scientific standards

- Prefer primary empirical sources for research claims.
- Never invent citations, methods, results, parameters, defaults, file paths, or project facts.
- Report exact p-values, effect estimates, confidence intervals, assumptions, and uncertainty when available and relevant.
- Nonsignificance does not establish the null.
- Evidence for absence requires a justified method such as equivalence testing with a smallest effect size of interest or an appropriate Bayesian analysis.
- Record and report outcome-dependent choices and deviations honestly.
- Methods and available software must not determine the scientific question.
